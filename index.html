    About Weatherpin

    Tap anywhere on the world map, search for a place, or use My location to see the weather there: now, the next 24 hours and 7 days, with air quality, UV, rain radar and weather warnings.

    Where the data comes from

    • Weather, UV, snow and sea, and the balloon's winds: Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0)
    • Place search: Open-Meteo, with place names from GeoNames (CC BY 4.0)
    • Names of tapped places: Nominatim, from © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL)
    • Air quality, pollen and greenhouse gases: the Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service, via Open-Meteo
    • Weather on a past day: ERA5 from the Copernicus Climate Change Service, via Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0)
    • Northern lights: NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center (OVATION model, public domain)
    • Atmospheric correction (EDM): calculated in the app with the formula Leica Geosystems publishes in its total station manuals (e.g. the TS13 user manual, Technical Data → Formulas; visible red laser, 658 nm), from the forecast's temperature, ground-level pressure and humidity. 0 ppm = 1013.25 hPa, 12 °C, 60 % humidity. Other makes and lasers use slightly different formulas, usually within a few ppm.
    • Elevation: from the Copernicus DEM (90 m grid), via Open-Meteo; on steep ground it can be off by tens of metres.
    • Fishing times: worked out in the app from the moon's position (solunar periods, as in fishing calendars): an old tradition, not a forecast of how fish bite.
    • Electric car range in the cold: typical figures from published cold-weather tests and fleet data (NAF's El Prix winter tests in Norway, AAA, Geotab), by the temperature now; not any particular car.
    • Map: © OpenStreetMap contributors, shown with Leaflet
    • Rain radar: Environment and Climate Change Canada over North America (Open Government Licence – Canada) and RainViewer elsewhere
    • Weather warnings: Environment and Climate Change Canada, the US National Weather Service, MET Norway (CC BY 4.0), SMHI (CC BY 4.0) and the Finnish Meteorological Institute (CC BY 4.0), for Canada, the US, Norway, Sweden and Finland. Finland's come through this site's own server, as FMI's feed can't be read by browsers directly; your location is not sent to it (the app checks which warnings cover the place itself)
    • Road conditions in Finland: Fintraffic / digitraffic.fi (CC BY 4.0), the main road nearest the place; downloaded for the whole country, so no location is sent

    Privacy

    • No account, no cookies and no tracking. Visits are counted anonymously with Cloudflare Web Analytics, without cookies or anything that identifies you.
    • Your favourites, start place (rounded to about 1 km), unit choices, light or dark appearance and colours, language, rain radar setting, which sections you've folded away, your quiz results (today's score, streak and best) and your balloon's name and best flight are saved only in this browser, on this device. So is your weather journey: points, badges and which roughly 50 km areas, countries and continents you've looked at. So is the weather of the last three places you looked at (rounded to about 1 km), so it can be shown without a connection.
    • To show the weather, the coordinates of the place you pick are sent to the weather services above, and for a tapped point also to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim, for its name. For Weather on a day, the day you pick is sent to Open-Meteo too, and not saved. Like any website, they, the map service and Cloudflare (which hosts the site) see your IP address.
    • Your location is used only when you press My location. It is saved only if you make that place your start place, and with its weather (above), rounded to about 1 km.
    • The page address shows the place you're looking at (rounded to about 1 km), so it can be bookmarked or shared. It stays in your browser unless you share the link.

    Use at your own risk

    • Forecasts can be wrong, and data can be late or missing. Don't rely on this app alone where safety is at stake, such as at sea, in the mountains or in severe weather. Follow the official warnings of your national weather service.
    • Air quality, pollen and UV are general information, not medical advice.
    • The northern lights chance is a model estimate for the next hour or so, and the aurora can change within minutes. Stargazing, golden hour and the other sky times are calculated from the sun, moon and cloud forecast, and are approximate.
    • The atmospheric correction for surveying instruments and the air values (pollutants, CO₂, methane) are model estimates, not measurements. Don't use them where accuracy matters without measuring on site.
    • Weather excuses, small talk and haiku are for fun. They're based on the forecast for the place; the results are not guaranteed. We take no responsibility if your boss doesn't accept the excuse, the conversation dies after one line, or your cat disagrees.
    • The app is free and provided as is, without any warranty. To the extent the law allows, no liability is accepted for any loss from using it.
    • The app is not affiliated with or endorsed by the data providers or instrument makers above.
